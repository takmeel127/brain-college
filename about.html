Our Story

The Brain College
Bhakkar

The Brain College Bhakkar is a leading vocational and computer education institution established in 2011. Located at Darya Khan Road, Bhakkar, we have been committed to providing high-quality, practical education to students from all walks of life.

Our institute was founded with a simple but powerful vision: to bridge the skills gap in Bhakkar by providing professional, certified training that helps students secure jobs in government and private sectors across Pakistan.

Over 15 years, we have trained more than 5000 students, maintained two modern computer labs — separate for male and female students — and built a team of highly qualified, experienced instructors.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, affordable, and nationally recognized vocational and computer education that equips students with practical skills for career success in both government and private sectors.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and impactful educational institution in Bhakkar — empowering every student with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to build a successful future.

Our Values

Excellence in education, integrity in everything we do, inclusivity for all students, practical learning over theory, and continuous improvement to meet industry standards.
